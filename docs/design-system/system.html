SPEC A

Tape and Thread

A made-to-measure fitting, not a dashboard. One tape shows where the work stands. One thread is held out to you: the next decision only you can make. Everything else is ink, paper and chalk. The tailoring lives in the visuals and never in the words. The product keeps its own vocabulary: Interview, Documents, Roadmap, Build, Try it.

Take every word away and you should still recognise the app by these five things.

1. The ink railAlways on the left, ink coloured.

2. The tapeA measure with a pin, in a hairline bar. It shows where the project really is.

3. One canary threadThe next decision. Only one per screen.

4. Pin-head status dotsSeven fixed states, each with a glyph.

Law 6 in detail: the No Dead Space rule

Measured on the 8px cell grid of the content box (main minus the sticky top bar and the strip, inset by --pm-content-pad on the top, left and right). Every rectangle of ground colour with nothing in it is found by the tool, at both viewports, and judged against the constants below. Run it from the pramaan-web folder:

node docs/design-system/tools/whitespace.mjs [files] [--rail expanded|collapsed] [--quiet]

With no files it takes every screens/*.html except the skeleton. It writes shots/whitespace/<screen>-<viewport>-first-annot.png (red is a violation, blue is classified breathing room) and report.json, and exits 1 when anything fails. The whole set is 34 screens at two viewports: 68 checks, 0 failing.

The thresholds (constants in the tool)

ConstantValueWhat it forbids
FIRST240 by 160L6.1: an empty rectangle this size or larger in the first viewport.
CORNER240 by 120L6.2: the empty rectangle anchored at the content box's top-right corner may not be both this wide and this tall.
SCROLL240 by 240L6.3: an empty rectangle this size or larger anywhere in the stitched whole scroll of a scroller.

Nobody widens a threshold or adds an allowlist row without a ruling (Ruling 9).

Measurement tolerances, so each allowlist row measures what it says

ToleranceWhat it lets the tool do

Risks and limits

SPEC H

Not in this system: mobile and tablet layouts (below 1024px the rail collapses and panels stack, nothing more), the marketing site, the dependency monitoring view beyond its Settings entry, an icon style beyond the 1.5px stroke set, live data (every screen is static), and any change to route structure or the wire vocabulary. Pass 2 also leaves undrawn a Lane B interview, documents and roadmap (open question D-P2-4) and the Lane B settings screen (specified, not drawn).

RiskGuard
