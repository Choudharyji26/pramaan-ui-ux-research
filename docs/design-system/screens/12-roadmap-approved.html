    • Log in with the right password and see your empty checklist

  2. Sprint 2 · Keep your list

    WaitingStarts after sprint 1 is accepted.

    You will be able to:

    • Rename a task and see the new name in the same placeYou said: 'add, rename, delete, and check off tasks' See that decision
    • Check a task and see it marked as done
    • Uncheck a done task and see it marked as not done
    • Delete a task, confirming first, and see only that task disappear
    • Cancel a delete and see the task stay
    • Try to open or rename someone else's task and see you are turned away
    • Change a task on one device and see the change on another device that is open

    You can try: rename, check off, uncheck and delete your tasks, and your list stays current while it is open. Nobody else can see or change your tasks.

    Then: try sprint 2, share what you found, accept it. Sprint 3 starts when you accept.

  3. Sprint 3 · Fresh checks every day

    WaitingStarts after sprint 2 is accepted.

    You will be able to:

    • Leave a checked list open through your local midnight and see the checks clear without reloadingYou said: 'checks that clear each day' See that decision
    • Open your list after a missed midnight and see the checks already cleared
    • Keep a second device open and see it show the reset too
    • Check a task after the reset and see it stay checked when the other device reaches midnight
    • Confirm all your tasks are still there in the same order after a reset

    You can try: start each local day with unchecked tasks: checks clear at midnight on an open list, or when you next open the list, while your tasks and their order stay.

    Then: try sprint 3, share what you found, accept it. Sprint 4 starts when you accept.

  4. Sprint 4 · Password recovery and every device

    WaitingStarts after sprint 3 is accepted.

    You will be able to:

    • Ask for a password recovery email and see it arrive
    • Try again after a failed recovery request
    • Follow the email link, set a new password, and log in with it
    • Open your checklist on a phone, a tablet and a computer and use the task controls on each
    • Add the checklist to your phone's home screen and open it from there
    • Judge whether the controls and task states are easy to understand on all three devices

    You can try: get back into your account after forgetting your password, and use and review the finished checklist on a phone, a tablet, a computer and your phone's home screen.

    Then: try sprint 4, share what you found, accept it.
