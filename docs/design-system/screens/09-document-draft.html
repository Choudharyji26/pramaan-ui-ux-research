One-sentence outcome

The one thing this product should do.

For two people sharing a screen, change a tic-tac-toe screen idea to a playable game where X starts, players take turns, the result is shown, and they can start a new game each time they play.

Evidence of the problem

What you told us that led to this brief.

  • Source: The founder's description and confirmed decisions.
  • What was observed: The founder asked for a playable tic-tac-toe screen for two people taking turns on the same screen, with X starting every game, a winner or draw shown at the end, no further moves after the result, and a new-game button available at any time.
  • Confidence / unknowns: These are settled product decisions; no existing screen or player research was provided.

Acceptance signal

The check you can run yourself to accept the result.

  • Reproducible product/technical check: On the browser preview, two people alternate moves; a win and a draw each show the correct result and prevent further moves; the new-game button clears the game during play and after a result, with X taking the first turn.
  • Subjective decision and named approver: None required for the stated outcome.

Constraints and non-goals

What the product must stay within, and what it will not do.

  • Constraint: This is a browser-based web app that works on phones, tablets, and computers and can be added to a phone's home screen; it is not a native or app-store app.
  • Constraint: Two people play on the same screen; X takes the first turn in every new game.
